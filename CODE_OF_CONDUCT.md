# Code of conduct

ra11y is built by people who want software to be usable by everyone. We hold ourselves to the same standard when we interact as contributors.

## Our commitment

We welcome participation from everyone, regardless of level of experience, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, disability, personal appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, age, religion, nationality, or any other dimension of identity. We are especially committed to creating an environment where disabled contributors are full participants — this project is about them.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind. Assume good faith.
- Be specific when you disagree. "This breaks the zero-dep invariant because X" is a conversation; "this is wrong" is a dead end.
- Credit others. Accessibility work is often invisible — name the people whose thinking you built on.
- Use inclusive language. Prefer "people with disabilities" or the community's preferred identity-first language where it is specified. When in doubt, ask.
- Respect boundaries. Not every conversation needs to happen.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or imagery
- Trolling or sustained disruption of discussion
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately via [GitHub's private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/vanctran/ra11y/security/advisories/new) (the same channel is used for conduct reports until a dedicated one exists). Reports will be handled confidentially.

Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.
