# 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum)

- **Level:** AA
- **Automatable:** partial
- **Spec:** <https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#contrast-minimum>

## Normative text

The visual presentation of text and images of text has a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text).
