# 1.4.6 Contrast (Enhanced)

- **Level:** AAA
- **Automatable:** partial
- **Spec:** <https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#contrast-enhanced>

## Normative text

The visual presentation of text and images of text has a contrast ratio of at least 7:1 (4.5:1 for large text).
