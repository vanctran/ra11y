# 1.4.10 Reflow

- **Level:** AA
- **Automatable:** partial
- **Spec:** <https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#reflow>

## Normative text

Content can be presented without loss of information or functionality, and without requiring two-dimensional scrolling, at widths down to 320 CSS pixels.
