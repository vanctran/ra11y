# 2.2.2 Pause, Stop, Hide

- **Level:** A
- **Automatable:** partial
- **Spec:** <https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#pause-stop-hide>

## Normative text

For moving, blinking, scrolling, or auto-updating information, users can pause, stop, or hide it.
