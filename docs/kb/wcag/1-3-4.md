# 1.3.4 Orientation

- **Level:** AA
- **Automatable:** partial
- **Spec:** <https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#orientation>

## Normative text

Content does not restrict its view and operation to a single display orientation unless a specific orientation is essential.
