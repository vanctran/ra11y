# 3.1.5 Reading Level

**Level:** AAA | **Standard:** WCAG 2.2
**Spec:** <https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#reading-level>

## Why manual

Assessing reading level requires natural-language processing and knowledge of the content's target audience. Static analysis cannot reliably measure readability grade level, identify whether prose is above lower secondary education level, or verify whether a simpler alternative exists.

## What to verify

> "When text requires reading ability more advanced than the lower secondary education level after removal of proper names and titles, supplemental content, or a version using vocabulary and sentence structure of lower secondary education level, is available."

"Lower secondary education level" is approximately Grade 9 (ages 14–15) — the Flesch-Kincaid grade level 7–8 range. Proper names and titles are excluded from assessment. Compliance is achieved by either: (a) writing at or below the target level, or (b) providing a plain-language summary or alternative version alongside the complex text.

## Common failure modes

- Legal terms and conditions, privacy policies, or compliance disclosures with no plain-language summary.
- Medical instructions or clinical documentation without a patient-friendly version.
- Academic or technical articles published verbatim without an accessible abstract or summary.
- Product descriptions using marketing superlatives and jargon without plain explanation.
- Automated readability score accepted at face value without reviewing for domain jargon that inflates effective complexity.

## Attestation guidance

To attach evidence to an `attest` call (`ra11y-kb://criteria/wcag22/3.1.5`):

- Run readability analysis (Flesch-Kincaid, Gunning Fog, or equivalent) on each content-heavy page.
- Identify passages above Grade 9 level after stripping proper nouns and titles.
- For each such passage, confirm a plain-language alternative or summary is present and linked.
- Verify the alternative is findable from the complex-text page.

Evidence: readability scores per page, list of pages above threshold, plain-language alternatives provided.
