# 3.1.5 Reading Level (WCAG 2.1)

**Level:** AAA | **Standard:** WCAG 2.1
**Spec:** <https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/#reading-level>

Identical requirement to `wcag22:3.1.5`. See the canonical entry:
[docs/kb/criteria/wcag22/3.1.5-reading-level.md](../wcag22/3.1.5-reading-level.md)

## What to verify

> "When text requires reading ability more advanced than the lower secondary education level after removal of proper names and titles, supplemental content, or a version using vocabulary and sentence structure of lower secondary education level, is available."

Full attestation guidance: see `wcag22:3.1.5` entry above.
